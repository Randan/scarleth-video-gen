The camera is completely locked off on a tripod for the whole clip: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no push-in, no drift; the framing never changes. This is a painted artwork coming gently alive as a slow, calm, continuous ambient loop; its painting style, colours and lighting stay exactly as they are. Nothing new ever appears, nothing leaves, nothing is added; every object keeps its exact shape, size and position. Fine dust and tiny golden sparks float slowly through the beam of light, the light beam shimmers softly, smoke and haze drift slowly between the ruined buildings, and the green glow at the base of the red flower pulses softly. The red flower and its leaves tremble very gently in a faint breeze. The ruined buildings, the rock outcrop and its roots stay perfectly still and unchanged.
