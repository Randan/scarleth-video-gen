The camera is completely locked off on a tripod for the whole clip: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no push-in, no drift; the framing never changes. This is a painted artwork coming gently alive as a slow, calm, continuous ambient loop; its painting style, colours and lighting stay exactly as they are. Nothing new ever appears except the lightning, nothing leaves; every object keeps its exact shape, size and position. The flames of the two torches flicker and dance softly and tiny glowing embers float up from them. Fine dust motes drift slowly through the beams of light, the storm clouds behind the stone archway move slowly, and low mist swirls slowly across the stone floor. Now and then a real thunderstorm lightning strike flashes in the dark storm clouds behind the archway: a single bright, sharp, jagged, branching bolt of white-violet electric light that cracks across the sky in an instant, lights up the clouds and vanishes within a fraction of a second. Lightning bolts never sway, wave or drift; they are not threads or webs. The stone bell with its skulls, clawed legs and the Medusa face relief, the stone pillars and the archway stay perfectly still, solid and unchanged.
