The camera is completely locked off on a tripod for the whole clip: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no push-in, no drift; the framing never changes. This is a painted artwork coming alive as a continuous ambient loop; its painting style, colours and lighting stay exactly as they are. Nothing new ever appears, nothing leaves, nothing is added; every solid object keeps its exact shape, size and position. On the left the whole dark smoke cloud that fills the left sky billows and rolls steadily from left to right across the sky at a clearly visible pace — every part of the smoke moves, the large dark cloud as well as the thin plumes rising from the fires. The fires flicker and the flames dance, glowing embers and sparks drift upward. The jagged rift down the middle glows and pulses softly with molten red-orange light. On the right the soft clouds drift slowly, the meadow grass and the leaves of the trees sway softly in a light breeze. The few tiny birds in the sky stay exactly where they are as still specks — they do not fly, move, merge or multiply. The overall light stays constant: the sun does not brighten, no sunbeams or light rays appear, the sky keeps its brightness and colour. The tank, the soldier, the ruined buildings, the city skyline and the hills stay perfectly still.
