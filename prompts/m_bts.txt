The camera is completely locked off on a tripod for the whole clip: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no push-in, no drift; the framing never changes. This is a painted artwork coming gently alive as a slow, calm, continuous ambient loop; its painting style, colours and lighting stay exactly as they are. Nothing new ever appears, nothing leaves, nothing is added; every object keeps its exact shape, size and position. The flames of the two torches flicker and dance softly and tiny glowing embers float up from them. Fine dust motes drift slowly through the beams of light, the clouds behind the stone archway move very slowly, and low mist swirls slowly across the stone floor. The stone bell with its skulls, clawed legs and the Medusa face relief, the stone pillars and the archway stay perfectly still, solid and unchanged.
