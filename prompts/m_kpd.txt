The camera is completely locked off on a tripod for the whole clip: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no push-in, no drift; the framing never changes. This is a painted artwork coming gently alive as a slow, calm, continuous ambient loop; its painting style, colours and lighting stay exactly as they are. Nothing new ever appears, nothing leaves, nothing is added; every object keeps its exact shape, size and position. On the left the fires flicker and the flames dance softly, glowing embers and sparks drift slowly upward, and the dark smoke rolls slowly across the sky. The jagged rift down the middle glows and pulses softly with molten red-orange light. On the right the soft clouds drift very slowly, the sun rays shimmer gently, the meadow grass and the leaves of the tree sway softly in a light breeze, and the few small birds far away glide slowly. The tank, the soldier, the ruined buildings, the city skyline and the hills stay perfectly still.
